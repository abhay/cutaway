Privacy policy

Updated October 12, 2026

Website and plugin

Cutaway has no accounts, analytics, ads, cookies, or browser storage. Controls run locally. Tool calls and shared or downloaded views send model settings to our server; we don’t save tool requests or generated files.

Our host, Cloudflare, may retain IP addresses and request records under its privacy policy.

ChatGPT

“Discuss this view” sends settings to ChatGPT; the drawing skill doesn’t send custom files to Cutaway. OpenAI’s privacy policy and your account settings apply.

Support

GitHub issues are public. X, GitHub, and linked websites follow their own policies.
